Origins and development

A distinct history within biomagnetism

Two ideas need to be separated even though they use the same word. Scientific biomagnetism studies magnetic fields produced by living organisms and other bioelectromagnetic phenomena. The Biomagnetic Pair is a specific therapeutic proposal associated with Isaac Goiz Durán.

Organizations connected with the method describe Goiz as developing the Biomagnetic Pair in 1988 and later founding the Centro de Investigación de Biomagnetism Médico. This section presents that history as the practice’s own account, without turning its therapeutic explanations into scientific consensus.

01 · Biomagnetism

Context and terminology

Biomagnetism is a broad term. In science, it can refer to the study of magnetic properties and signals associated with biological systems, including measurements produced by tissues and physiological processes. In another context, “medicinal biomagnetism” is the name used by institutions and practitioners for a therapeutic proposal that places magnets at specific body locations. The two ideas share the word “magnetism”, but they are not automatically the same field.

02 · Biomagnetism

How the therapeutic proposal is presented

Instituto Par Magnético describes Medicinal Biomagnetism as a technique using pairs of magnets and screening protocols. Its educational materials show magnet kits, anatomical maps and specific training for applying the technique. This helps explain how the practice is taught by an institution in the field; it does not, by itself, establish clinical effectiveness.

03 · Biomagnetism

What research can investigate

Scientific research on magnetism and health involves very different questions. Some studies measure magnetic fields generated by the body or use magnetic fields in medical technologies such as magnetic resonance imaging. Research on magnet-based therapies, however, needs to evaluate clinical outcomes directly, using methods that can separate intervention effects from expectations, context and other factors. Results from one area should therefore not be used to automatically validate another.

04 · Biomagnetism

A balanced reading of evidence

For a responsible school-level reading, it is important to ask which technique was studied, what the comparison was, which outcome was measured and whether the study can be reproduced. The existence of magnets in medical equipment is a scientific fact, but this does not mean that small magnets used in a therapeutic session produce the same effects. This page presents the practice as a subject of study and describes its claims without turning them into medical conclusions.

05 · Biomagnetism

Care for a responsible experience

For any session, it is useful to understand the method being offered, the practitioner’s training and the purpose of the session. People who use implanted medical devices or have received specific guidance about exposure to magnetic fields should speak with their healthcare team before any practice involving magnets. Biomagnetism should not replace medical evaluation, diagnosis or treatment when these are needed.

Care and limits · Biomagnetism

Responsible use and precautions

When a session uses therapeutic magnets, it is important to know which method is being offered and who is conducting it. Magnets should not be presented as a substitute for diagnosis, treatment, or medical follow-up. People who use implanted medical devices or have specific clinical guidance should talk to a health professional before taking part in a practice involving magnetic fields.

  • Identify the method and the practitioner’s training.
  • Do not stop medicines or treatments recommended by a healthcare team.
  • Mention implanted medical devices and other relevant clinical guidance before a session.

03 · Biomagnetism

How a session may take place

In a session described by practitioners of Biomagnetic therapy, the appointment may begin with a discussion of the person's goals and, depending on the method used, continue with body assessment and the placement of pairs of magnets at points defined by the protocol. The format varies among practitioners. Describing a session explains how the practice is performed; it does not mean that its mechanisms or therapeutic benefits are established for all conditions.
