Restrictions

This page gathers practical guidance for understanding each approach with clarity, respect and attention to quality.

01

Know the practice

Understand how the tradition describes itself before comparing it with research.

02

Look at quality

Training, hygiene, materials and communication are part of a well-organized experience.

03

Keep care central

Complementary practices do not replace healthcare when it is needed.

Before a session, identify which modality is being offered and which materials will be used. Instituto Par Magnético describes magnet use within its own screening protocols. People with implanted medical devices or specific guidance about magnetic fields should discuss the practice with their healthcare team first.

✦ Identify the method and materials used before the session.

Quality depends on hygiene, appropriate materials and training. WHO highlights procedures, facilities and safety as components of good practice. Tell the practitioner about medicines, health conditions and pregnancy so the session can be planned appropriately.

✦ Prioritize hygiene, appropriate materials and qualified practice.

The approach is officially described as spiritual and educational, focused on personal insight and development. A session should not be presented as diagnosis, psychotherapy, prescription or disease treatment. When there is a clinical need, the appropriate care should be provided by a qualified professional.

✦ Keep the approach within the spiritual and educational context in which it is presented.

Asking about training, session goals, materials and follow-up helps create a clearer experience. It is also useful to separate descriptions of a tradition, statements from an organization and findings demonstrated by independent research.

✦ Ask about the purpose, training and follow-up to understand the experience.
Additional information

Attention to detail makes information more useful

The purpose of this page is not to create fear. It helps organize simple and important questions before a session: who conducts it, how the practice is presented, which materials are used and how it relates to healthcare.
